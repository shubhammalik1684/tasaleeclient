We test the concrete, we do not admire the kitchen

An architect attends every premium file with instruments, not opinions. Three readings go into the report with the photographs that produced them, dated and located in the unit.

Inside the shell
Settlement cracks over openings, deflection in the slab, water tracking in the wet walls, and what has been plastered over.
Ultrasonic pulse velocity
A pulse through a column tells you the concrete quality inside it. Under 3.0 km per second is a finding, not an opinion.
Moisture and cover
Moisture readings near every wet area and a cover meter over the reinforcement, because rusting steel is what actually brings a slab down.
Instrument readings are taken by a registered architect. They describe the condition on the day of the visit and do not replace a structural engineer's certificate where one is required.

Questions buyers ask

Are you connected to a builder or a broker?

No. We take no commission on any transaction and we are not paid by any seller. Our only income is the report fee, which is why the report can say do not buy this.

What if the report tells me to walk away?

Then it has done its job and saved you far more than it cost. Roughly one file in six carries a defect serious enough that we recommend against proceeding until it is resolved.

Can I use this to get a home loan?

The premium report includes a valuation letter written for a lender, and the title findings rest on certified copies rather than on unsigned online records.

How current is the information?

Every source is dated in the report. Land records and court searches are run within days of delivery, and we state the exact date each one was pulled.

What kinds of property do you cover?

Open plots, builder floors, apartments and commercial units, across Gurugram, south and west Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad and Sonipat.

Do you inspect before I make an offer, or after?

Before. The point is to give you the number and the leverage while you can still use them.
